## Introduction

Lately, killing the main character has become fashionable in books. This book is no exception by any means.

Here he lies before you in a graceless and rather ugly pose, arms spread wide as if trying to embrace the cruel world that killed him — our main hero.

Quite possibly, looking at this scene, you are having thoughts along the lines of:

"Maxim died — well, never mind[^1]. I don't even really know him. What's so 'main' or 'heroic' about him anyway?"

If that is the case, then I have something to say in response:

- This is not Maxim.
- Almost every death is a tragedy for someone. No matter how unknown that main character was.
- No need to rush. I'm about to tell you everything about him. Hopefully, by the end of it, you'll be somewhat ashamed of yourself.

[^1]: A Russian saying. Literally: "Maxim died — and to hell with him." About how little people actually care about each other.
